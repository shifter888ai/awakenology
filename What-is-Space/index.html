What Is Space?

YWS

(First edition: 2019)

For experienced practitioners of out-of-body exploration, many have personally observed and experienced that water is a living organism, fire is a living organism, earth is a living organism, and air is also a living organism. Different elements are different forms of life. Their modes of consciousness, thought, and emotion differ greatly from those of plants and animals. They are profoundly different forms of life, with very different purposes. The author also refers to modern scientific experiments as having identified consciousness in water and emotions in plants.

However, the author’s discovery over recent decades is that space itself is also a living organism.

Space appears invisible to the naked eye. Yet, when viewed with what the author calls the “spiritual eye,” different states of living bodies can be observed within it.

A. Observation Methods and Phenomena

Observation method

  1. Prepare to enter a meditative state (mental preparation).
  2. Lie flat on your back and enter a trance state.
  3. Shift the focus of consciousness from the physical body to the spiritual body, while keeping the spiritual body within the physical body.
  4. Adjust the “frequency” of consciousness to a “stellar level” (still within the quasi-material realm). The author reports being able to see stars directly through the ceiling.
  5. At this point, the visual frequency of the spiritual body is described as higher than the frequency range of Earth’s physical matter, allowing vision to “pass through” the ceiling, regardless of the number of floors.
  6. The two cloud layers above Earth’s surface can also be seen through.
  7. Key point: adjusting the “frequency” of consciousness is described as a subtle skill requiring regular practice and accumulated experience. With repeated practice, the target frequency can gradually be reached. Throughout the process, remain calm, avoid deliberate thinking, and focus on objective observation and frequency adjustment.

Recorded observations

  1. Between stars, the author reports observing space as being composed of a dark-red, fluid-like stream of consciousness. It forms a relatively stable spatial fluid, which the author calls a “space-consciousness-stream organism,” “space-consciousness body,” or “space organism” (hereafter, “spatial fluid” or “space body”).
  2. The dark-red stream is described as continuously flowing and forming small vortices. These vortices are identified in the account as stars (or “stellar consciousness-stream organisms”). Smaller micro-vortices continually appear around stars and are described as forming planetary bodies (or “planetary consciousness-stream organisms”) that orbit them.
  3. The flow speed and color vary slightly by region, ranging from dark red to bright red. According to the account, these differences produce vortices with different types, shapes, colors, densities, rotation speeds, structural layers, energy modes, consciousness levels, light colors, and frequencies. These variations form different kinds of stars and their associated satellites.
  4. Very large compound vortices are described as forming galaxies (or “galactic consciousness-stream organisms”).
  5. The density of a vortex is described as clearly greater than that of the surrounding spatial stream.
  6. The vortices of galaxies, stars, planets, and satellites move with the consciousness stream of space, somewhat like fish living in water.
  7. Vortices are not necessarily red; the author reports many colors, including black and white. Compared with the scale of the dark-red stream, the vortices are very small. Micro-vortices are even harder to notice and are described as lying close to, and entirely within, the central region of their main vortices.
  8. The account describes a very large difference between the consciousness-energy level of a planetary body and that of the space body. Compared with the dark-red stream making up the space body, organisms on a planet are described as extremely small and passive, and therefore as having very limited understanding of the space body.
  9. The difference between the consciousness-energy levels of a stellar body and a space body is described as relatively smaller. Organisms on stars are therefore said to have some understanding of the space body and to be able to interact with it.

The author says this interaction between stellar beings and the space body has been observed many times over several decades. The following case is offered as an example.

B. Observation Case: Interaction Between the Sun Being and the Space Body

Observations of the solar beings

During one observation of the spatial fluid, the author reports seeing two solar beings come to Earth.

The two beings drew a “fiber-optic line” made of sunlight from the Sun and slid down it to the surface of Earth, stopping in mid-air above the second cloud layer. The descent took only one or two seconds. From their perspective, the distance between the Sun and Earth seemed almost nonexistent. Because they moved so quickly, the author says the first observation did not reveal clearly how they passed through the dark-red stream.

  1. The author attempted to greet them through telepathy, but they did not respond and appeared not to notice the author.
  2. Through telepathic observation, the author perceived that humans on Earth appeared to them like ants.
  3. The author then sought to understand their purpose and perceived that they were rescue personnel sent to assist Earth. According to the account, they come at irregular intervals when summoned by Earth to help repair it.
  4. The author describes the “repair” as addressing small “black holes” in Earth’s energy field: black energy vortices that produce spatial curvature and draw nearby streams and consciousness bodies into another part of space, creating a vacuum-like effect in the affected region. The account says Earth’s spirit can adjust small vortices itself, but may call on the Sun’s spirit when one grows larger. These vortices are described as having a much greater impact on Earth than human activity, which is said to affect only a small part of the surface.
  5. The two rescue workers were described as construction workers. A solar spacecraft was bringing the “supplies” they needed.

After following their apparent train of consciousness, the author reports seeing a solar spacecraft emerge from the Sun’s surface, preparing to cross space and deliver supplies to them.

Observations of the solar spacecraft

The spacecraft was described as elongated and oval-shaped, with evenly spaced orange-red and bright-red stripes connecting its two ends, somewhat like the striped underside of a whale, but in different colors.

The spacecraft was at the Sun’s outer layer and preparing to cross space. The author records the process as follows:

  1. The spacecraft first communicated with the dark-red stream of consciousness that was said to compose space.
  2. The dark-red stream then separated, creating a “gap” without the stream between the Sun and Earth (described as the disappearance of space).
  3. The starting point of this gap was where the spacecraft was located at the Sun’s outer layer; its endpoint was beside the two solar beings above Earth’s second cloud layer.
  4. The space body between the two points disappeared, and the two points were “compressed” until they were almost one point.
  5. At that moment, the spacecraft moved from the starting point to the endpoint instantaneously.
  6. The two points then separated, and the dark-red stream rejoined and returned to its original state (described as the restoration of space).
  7. The entire process took only two or three seconds. Most of the time was spent communicating with the dark-red stream, waiting for it to separate and bring the two points together, and then waiting for it to reunite and separate the points. The actual movement between the Sun and Earth was said to take almost no time.

Observations of spatial compression

  1. When the “surrounding space” between two points was compressed, the grid-like flow lines of the spatial stream were also compressed and curved. Objects within it were likewise compressed and curved. However, they were said to feel no deformation because their spatial density and relative reference points did not change. The author compares this to a rising water level lifting a boat: the boat’s relative position in the water remains unchanged.
  2. Even when the two points were compressed to almost one point and the density of the “surrounding space” approached infinity, the reference points changed at the same time, so objects within it still did not perceive the compression or curvature of the spatial grid.
  3. When the “surrounding space” beyond the two points was “diluted,” its density decreased and it curved. Objects within it were likewise said not to perceive the deformation of their own space. Light was also described as being deformed.
  4. Only the two points themselves were merged into almost one point, allowing the spacecraft to move instantaneously. The spacecraft was said to perceive almost no movement itself, but to confirm the relocation of its spatial position by observing the instantaneous change in the surrounding space. In other words, it moved an extremely small distance between the starting and ending points after they had almost merged. Through immediate telepathic communication with the space-consciousness body, the spacecraft also confirmed the subtle change in position and completion of the “transit.”

In brief, the author describes space as curving, with objects and reference objects curving along with it. When an object curves together with its surroundings, it does not feel the curvature; when the reference objects curve as well, the curvature cannot be measured. The author summarizes this as “two negatives make a positive.” After the distances in the spatial grid are “compressed,” the phenomenon described as “instantaneous movement” (transit) occurs.

Other observations

  1. The solar spacecraft was also described as a living organism. Looking through its body with the “spiritual eye,” the author reports seeing no other organisms or solar beings inside it, and no cockpit. It appeared to operate its body independently. It had no internal organs; its hollow interior served as a cargo space filled with supplies and tools. Its body moved subtly, almost imperceptibly, in a biological rhythm. Throughout the transit, its consciousness was said to communicate actively with the Sun itself and the spatial stream, as well as with the two solar beings. Its state of mind remained stable.
  2. A control center inside the Sun was described as remotely controlling the spacecraft through telepathic communication. The starting position, route, destination, and arrangements for the journey—including communications with the space body and Earth body—were said to have been programmed in advance at the control center. The journey was operated mainly by remote control, with the spacecraft-like organism primarily executing the program. The author characterizes this as a highly automated process.

C. Additional Observations of the Spatial Fluid

The author says there were many other observations of the spatial fluid over time, broadly similar to those above, and lists several key points:

  1. The stream of consciousness appears endless, like an ocean without boundaries, filling the entire universe—or, conversely, forming the entire universe.
  2. Later observations led the author to conclude that, in the early stages of creating any tangible thing, consciousness creates the space-consciousness either beforehand or at the same time. The formation of a space-consciousness body is also the formation of a world.
  3. Spatial bodies created by individual consciousness are described as less stable and liable to gradually disappear if not maintained. Spatial bodies created by collective consciousness are described as more stable and liable to grow spontaneously, like something fermenting, without people noticing.

D. Experiment: Creating the Smallest Space

Experimental procedure:

  1. Close the eyes, relax, and enter a trance state.
  2. Enter an out-of-body state, separating the spiritual body from the physical body.
  3. While out of the body, adjust the “frequency” of consciousness and instruct the spiritual body not to enter any known world. No other intention or thought should be present; the state should be extremely pure.
  4. If what leaves the body is only a point of consciousness rather than the spiritual body, return to the body and try again until the entire spiritual body can drift out.
  5. If the subconscious directs the spiritual body into a particular world, adjust the “frequency” of consciousness to leave that realm, without entering another one.
  6. After repeated attempts, gradually eliminate interference from the subconscious and the tendency to follow whatever arises. The spiritual body may then remain in a temporarily stable state without entering any realm.
  7. At this point, try to let the spiritual body drift naturally backward (without forceful intention). The author reports finding insufficient space for it to move.
  8. Looking around with the “spiritual eye,” the author reports darkness in every direction. The space containing the physical body was only slightly larger than the body itself, approximately two or three metres across, including the spiritual body.
  9. A little intention was needed to push the spiritual body backward. The author reports that the space behind it expanded slightly in response to the intention, allowing the spiritual body to drift a little.
  10. When the intention stopped, the space no longer expanded and the spiritual body could not move.
  11. When the intention resumed, the space expanded a little more and the spiritual body drifted a little farther.
  12. Repeat the experiment, keeping the intention pure.
  13. The author describes this as the smallest space encountered: only slightly larger than the primary creator body.

The author’s stated conclusions from the experiment:

  1. The space body is created before the physical form.
  2. The movement of a physical form is limited by the capacity of the space body.
  3. One’s own consciousness can create a space of its own.

Further observation: Collective consciousness can create a space belonging to the collective.

E. Further Observation: The Largest Space

Through years of out-of-body observations and experiments, the author gradually arrived at the following descriptions:

  1. The largest space: The largest space is what we ordinarily call “the universe.”
  2. Composite space: The universe in which humanity lives is described as a “composite space”—many spaces of different sizes overlapping one another to form a “multidimensional composite space body,” which continues to change.
  3. Composite universes: The author reports that many other universes similar to ours exist. When becoming one with the Source, the author says these universes and the processes by which they are created and change can be observed clearly. This collection is described as a “universe collective” or “composite universes.”

F. Historical Observations of the Spatial Stream

The author later found references in traditional Western spiritual-practice materials to a fluid-like stream of consciousness said to compose space. Ancient practitioners reportedly called it “ether” or “aether.” At times, “ether” was also used as another word for “space.”
