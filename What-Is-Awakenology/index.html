What Is Awakenology?

Awakenology is an experimental science focused on developing and testing methodologies to awaken individuals from states of psychological hypnosis. At its core is awakenosis - a practical process of de-hypnosis that disrupts patterns of hypnotic influence and reverses conditioned mental states. The goal is to achieve an awakened state, defined by greater self-awareness, cognitive freedom, and personal autonomy.

Awakenology prioritizes hands-on experimentation to understand how hypnotic states function and how they can be effectively reversed. Drawing from psychology, neuroscience, and consciousness studies, the field focuses on real-world applications, from improving cognitive freedom to addressing more complex challenges, such as breaking the hypnotic cycle of reincarnation. Each method is rigorously tested for its ability to produce tangible results, making Awakenology a results-driven approach to expanding human consciousness and potential.

History of Awakenology?

  • 1979: Early practices and experiments began.
  • 2008: Public Q&A on Awakenology initiated.
  • 2017: Awakenology Academy (NPO) established, and the definition of the field was formalized.
