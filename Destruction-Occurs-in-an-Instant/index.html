Destruction Occurs in an Instant

YWS
(Second Edition, 2022-03-12)

 

How were the 3,000 team members from The Domain instantaneously captured 10,000 years ago on Earth?

At that time, to execute operations within the material realm and interact with it, 3,000 souls assumed physical bodies and entered Earth via spacecrafts.

These souls had varying levels of SoC (Strength of Consciousness): approximately one-third had an SoC below 9, while around two-thirds had an SoC above 9 (with roughly one-third having an SoC above 13).

When a soul occupies a physical body, its connected consciousness is influenced by the physical state of the body.

T-Group, the reincarnation system operator of Earth and neighbouring planets, observed this situation and planned to exploit it by engineering a surprise hypnotic assault.

 

 

A) Execution of the Surprise Attack

 

1. First Second: High-Frequency Bomb Deployment – This bomb instantly disintegrated the physical bodies of the 3,000 team members, ejecting their souls. The sudden shock caused the SoC level of these souls to drop to around 9. This occurred within one second.  

 

   - High-Frequency Bomb Mechanism – Mother and Sub-Bomb Sequence (cumulative effect over one to three seconds):  

     1. Mother Bomb: Upon detonation, it emitted high-frequency shockwaves that disrupted molecular structures, leading to the immediate disintegration of physical bodies and the expulsion of souls. (Duration: few milliseconds)  

     2. Sub-Bombs: The detonation of the mother bomb triggered sub-bombs that emitted numerous random-frequency waves. These waves penetrated the 3,000 disoriented souls, causing them to perceive an intrusion of foreign frequency into their souls. This induced a reflexive response to cleanse, thereby delaying their escape. During this delay, a second wave of superimposed illusion bombs was activated. (Duration: cumulative effect from one to three seconds)

 

2. Second Second: Illusion Bomb Deployment – This bomb generated an instantaneous illusion that the souls of the 3,000 members had been shattered. This illusion-induced confusion caused a further reduction in SoC to below level 9. This took place within one second.  

 

   - Illusion Bomb Mechanism – Chain Reaction Bombs (cumulative effect over one to three seconds):

     1. The first illusion bomb exploded, projecting the illusion that each soul has been shattered. During the interval where each soul questioned the illusion and attempted to dispel it, the second chain illusion bomb was triggered, reinforcing the illusion.  

     2. The second illusion bomb created a subsequent illusion of soul fragmentation. During the interval of doubt and attempt to dispel the illusion, a third chain illusion bomb was activated, further intensifying the illusion.  

     3. The third illusion bomb continued to reinforce the shattered soul illusion, extending the time required to dispel the illusion. Simultaneously, the third round of attack was initiated: a three-round superimposed high-voltage electric shock

 

3. Third Second: High-Voltage Electric Shock – This technique induced immediate memory disruption among the 3,000 souls, stabilizing their SoC below level 9. This occurred within one second.  

 

   - High-Voltage Electric Shock Mechanism – Wave-Like Shock (cumulative effect over one to three seconds):

     1. The first wave of high-voltage shocks produced a high-frequency oscillation, which disoriented the souls' consciousness. During this phase of disorientation, a second shock wave was administered.  

     2. The second wave utilized high-frequency oscillation to further disrupt the already disoriented consciousness, achieving two fracturing effects on the consciousness structure of attacked souls.

        - Memory Loss Effect: Memories are stored in specific consciousness structures, defined by particular frequencies. The disruption of these structures led to memory fragmentation and loss.

        - SoC Reduction Effect: SoC is a stable structure and spectrum. Disruption or partial dispersal of this structure resulted in a decrease in SoC.  

     3. The third wave reinforced the disruption effect, ensuring the souls remained in a prolonged state of disorientation, characterized by memory loss and reduced SoC.

 

In just three seconds, the hypnotic capture was successfully executed.

 

The 3,000 dazed souls were placed in illusion bottles, taken to several hypnotic base laboratories, and locked in electronic illusion cages, awaiting experimentation and reincarnation.

 

 

B) The Only Successful Escape

 

During the three rounds of attacks, three souls attempted to escape:

 

- One was recaptured by the first containment perimeter shortly after attempting to escape.

- One managed to travel a certain distance but was intercepted by the second or third containment perimeter (specific details remain unclear).  

- Only one soul successfully evaded capture, breaching all three containment perimeters and tracking systems.

 

The escaped soul's composition was as follows:

 

- It was a composite soul, primarily composed of three souls.  

  1. Soul 1: The primary consciousness, an ancient entity, and one of the four team leaders, with an SoC of about 14. He is also a co-founder of The Domain.

  2. Soul 2: A subconscious of Soul 1, also the parent soul of Soul 1, with an SoC around 15. This soul was also the parent soul of other 1,560 team members.

  3. Soul 3: Another subconscious of Soul 1. It was a composite soul, formed by the integration of three or four souls, with an SoC in the range of 13-14.

 

The overall SoC was around14, which was at the upper limit of T-Group's hypnotic technology at that time.

 

The changing curve of SoC during the process of being ambushed and escaping:

 

1. During the first second of the surprise attack, his SoC was dropped from 14 to around 9.  

2. Over the three seconds of continuous illusion breaking, the SoC recovered from 9 to about 10.  

3. At the moment of escape, the SoC further recovered to around 12.

 

The escaped soul didn't know where the 3,000 team members' souls were taken to when he returned to his military base.

 

 

C) The Three-Second Lesson – Combat of Illusion Creation and Breaking

 

A split-second decision in battle comes from years of practice.

 

You need to develop constant awareness, all day, every day, 24 hours.

23 hours world fail.

23 hours 59 minutes and 57 seconds would also fail.

If you can’t react within 3 seconds, you're dead. (And this was the old technology from 10,000 years ago! The technology has now advanced to 1 second.)

 

Throughout history, very few have escaped the hypnotic reincarnation.

Do you understand why now?

[Postscript]

 

Ironically, the engineering manager leading T-Group's assault on The Domain team was actually an undercover agent from The Domain. To avoid exposing her identity, she had sealed her memories of The Domain and was unaware she was attacking her own people. Once her memories were restored, she realized the truth. Consequently, she was exposed as an undercover agent, captured, tortured, subjected to extreme hypnosis by T-Group, and subsequently imprisoned on Earth, where she remains to this day.
